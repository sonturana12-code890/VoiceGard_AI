Clarity & Security

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AI voice cloning, deepfake spoofing detection, and deployment.

How does VoiceGuard AI detect synthetic voices that sound 100% human?
Generative models (like ElevenLabs, VALL-E, and Bark) reconstruct audio using neural vocoders. Even when the audio sounds flawless to human ears, neural vocoders leave subtle mathematical anomalies: imperfect phase alignment between harmonics, high-frequency spectral roll-off, and unnatural formants. VoiceGuard AI analyzes these sub-audible artifacts with graph attention networks (AASIST).
What audio formats and file sizes are supported?
VoiceGuard AI supports all standard audio formats including .WAV, .MP3, .MPEG, .MPG, .FLAC, .OGG, .M4A, .AAC, and .WEBM up to 25 MB per sample. Files are validated using magic byte headers to prevent malicious payloads, converted to mono, and resampled to 16kHz for uniform neural processing.
Does VoiceGuard AI fabricate predictions if a model is not connected?
Never. In accordance with strict cybersecurity principles, when an AI inference checkpoint is not yet loaded, the system explicitly returns AI_MODEL_NOT_CONFIGURED. We never simulate or hallucinate fake detection probabilities.
Can I enroll and verify authorized speakers?
Yes. The platform includes a dedicated Speaker Verification module powered by ECAPA-TDNN embeddings. Organizations can register voiceprints for authorized executives, verify caller identity, and verify explicit biometric consent.
How can VoiceGuard AI be integrated into our contact center?
VoiceGuard AI exposes a high-throughput, low-latency REST API (`/api/audio/upload`, `/api/speaker/verify`, and `/api/threats`). Inbound SIP or WebRTC audio streams can be mirrored to the ingestion endpoint for asynchronous or synchronous threat scoring.
What compliance and audit trails are generated?
Every analysis generates a deterministic SHA-256 fingerprint, cryptographic event log, and timestamped forensic summary. These audit records can be downloaded individually or exported in bulk via CSV for compliance under ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II guidelines.
